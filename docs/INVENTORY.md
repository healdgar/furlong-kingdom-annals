# Livestock repair — engine baseline livestock-1, 3 October 2026

This intentionally changes future economic and demographic outcomes relative to
`9753322dea76eeca750a928e9133cf38d17f211d`. Existing person, life and annal
records remain intact. Seed/command saves regenerated by the new engine follow
its corrected outcomes; old snapshot archives retain their original quantities
and source identity. This is not an archive migration or a performance-only change.

## First violating transactions

Fresh hardware-GPU Chrome, sea seed/fate 1001, AD 850:

- Original parent, day 20: `takeHorses` requisitions one horse without removing
  local title. Porthcrug has 18.745237122178807 physical horses and
  19.745237122178807 claims immediately after the transaction. This is the
  earliest original-parent violation under the combined title oracle.
- Repair-stage source, day 41: `herdInit` allocates household animals without surrendering existing
  sale title. Lignonne has 204.9716049064345 physical sheep but
  358.70030858626035 combined private and sale claims. Initial allotment now
  transfers the corresponding fraction of existing title.
- Original parent, day 500: a sheep shipment leaves Ponte di Cala. Physical sheep decline from
  11.179889427201612 to 4.0261877627808325; private claims remain
  7.228924178825603 pending the next herd synchronization.
- Original parent, day 502: `moveHouseholdGoods` resets `_herdLast` to the smaller physical count
  without first applying that pending loss. Claims now permanently exceed
  physical animals. Subsequent birth scaling magnifies phantom claims, and
  `herdProduce` produces grain and wool from them.

These traces use different boundaries and repair stages: day 20 counts every
animal title; day 502 isolates private byres. Movement now synchronizes both communities before transferring title and
physical animals, then records the resulting physical baseline.

A second trace exposed imported animals being mistaken for births:
`herdSync` multiplied private claims by every positive physical delta.
Rougecastel's physical sheep rose from 8.543860228727755 to 49.513826339382035;
28.051600269091477 private sheep became 162.56610326973734.
`herdBirth` now credits actual births explicitly. Imports and purchased title
cannot trigger a second birth allocation.

Another trace exposed a custody error in annual purchases: a transaction in
Lignonne credited the household head's home byre at Rougecastel. Local
transactions now pass their custody site explicitly; the yearly herd census
reads that site's owner ledger, including remote owners. Canonical market
mutation also uses owner account keys; shared displayed seller names cannot
redirect a write into another household's inventory.

Culls, siege deaths, murrain and raids now remove animal title with physical
beasts through `herdLoss`. Lord byres' winter losses remove offered/held claims,
preventing later purchases of dead animals. Private winter losses stay with the
byre whose hay ran short. Missing animal fields no longer become NaN through
`undefined -= 0`; corrupt non-finite claims throw without erasing their evidence.
Cancelled shipments of at most half a unit now retain purchased stock under
the merchant: the former cancellation subtracted its physical quantity twice
while dropping the cargo. Payment remains a conserved purchase. Mustered
horses surrender local title while remaining physically counted in the host.
Pooled settlement transfers carry private animal title proportionally, including
tiny balances. Purchases and consumption retain every positive residual;
reconciliation no longer deletes positive offers below 0.001 units. Herd
synchronization no longer skips small nonzero losses. These are intentional
changes to the former numerical truncation rules.

## Checks and limits

`node --test tools/inventory.test.mjs` exercises the precise export → movement →
birth sequence, actual births versus imports, initial allotment, small shipment cancellation,
muster and raid conservation, sale/private
losses, tiny balances, migration, remote inheritance and corrupt claims.
`node --test tools/*.test.mjs` includes existing ownership, provisioning, life,
history, routing, advisor and script syntax checks.

`node tools/inventory-trace.mjs OUT candidate|GIT_REF YEARS SEED sea|land` runs
fresh isolated hardware-GPU Chrome and records source identity, annual statistics
and the first non-finite or over-physical combined animal title claim at herd/ownership
boundaries. The floating-point error bound is 32 × machine epsilon × the
largest observed quantity for that community/good × owner count. Using the
ending quantity alone incorrectly rejects cancellation: moving
4.477105034977209 physical sheep against a claim one ULP larger leaves a
8.881784197001252e-16 residual claim. The audit retains that evidence; the
engine never truncates it to satisfy the oracle. Physical dispatch can temporarily precede monthly ownership
synchronization; the audit distinguishes that pending interval from a baseline
reset which makes the discrepancy permanent. It is an independent recomputation,
not a quantity cap or a reconciliation used to repair the simulated world.

The repair introduces no capacity limits and changes no history-retention rule.
It does not establish complete goods custody equality, monetary reconciliation,
archive continuation or renderer-independent replay. Those require separate
integrated checks. Existing unnamed/unoffered remainder belongs to the lord;
full physical lot accounting remains separate work.

The soak money oracle now pairs each declared prepaid `<cause*` payout with its
synthetic negative `<>lost` mirror. On the original parent, seed 1001 sea first
failed at day 11 garrison pay: total purses changed by -1.46e-11 while the old
oracle expected +5.311443334054959 coins. The source debits the lord/crown
before paying townsmen; this was flow interpretation, not minting or an omitted
purse. The corrected original-parent phase audit completes day 360 without a
phase residual above 0.01. This is not an annual or mature-world clearance.
Twelve pure oracle fixtures retain detection of unfunded null payouts, missing
upstream debits and omitted recipient purses, and cover external inflow/outflow,
ordinary transfers, prepaid wages, looted-coin redistribution and null recipients.
The annual 0.01 threshold remains. Engine money transactions are unchanged.

`tools/soak.mjs --inventory 1` enables independent checks before and after each
whole simulated day. It validates finite nonnegative stores and animal claims;
combined livestock claims cannot exceed physical beasts within the observed
input-magnitude floating bound. Unoffered lord remainder is reported separately,
so this is not a daily title-equality requirement. The bound retains positive tiny
claims; it never mutates quantities. Canonical storage checks independently sum
lots, exclude transit from settlement stores, validate forward/reverse indices,
unique lot IDs, active compatible locations, capacity and existing total/occupancy
caches. Corruption fixtures cover each check and tiny/cancellation/transit cases.

These are matching and validity checks, not a complete production/consumption
conservation ledger. They do not establish that correctly matched quantities
were legitimately created, or that every historical tiny balance was retained.
Event replay and explicit transaction fixtures address different questions.
The first inventory failure appears in annual JSON and fails `inventoryMatched`;
the audit preserves it through later years. Waiting between days uses the sticky
fault-aware outcome `wait()`, including the independent storage-event harness.
