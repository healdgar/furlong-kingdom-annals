# The screen, for players and advisors

## The top bar

The date (click it for the **timeline**), the treasury of the crown or house you
rule (click it for the **kingdom accounts**), **Pause**, the **speed** list and
**Menu**. Clicking *Furlong* shows what the game is.

## Speeds

The speed list runs from Life pace to Reel years; **Pause** stops the calendar.
An advisor reads the speed as a number:

| Speed | Name | Pace |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Paused | the calendar stands still |
| 1 | Normal | half a day a second |
| 2 | Fast | two days a second |
| 3 | Very fast | eight days a second |
| 4 | Fastest | thirty days a second |
| 5 | Reel years | a year a second, the map redrawn monthly |
| 6 | Life pace | a day in half an hour: the folk at their work |

Keys: `Space` pause, `1`–`5` the speeds above, `L` Life pace.

## Menu

**Menu** opens the realm's panels; each opens in the one card beside the map:

- **Crown**: to watch, choose a campaign (100, 200 or 400 years) and take the
  crown or a great house. When you rule it is the court, in four views:
  *Overview* (revenue, the campaign's paths, ambitions), *Governance* (your
  towns' orders, works and storage), *Orders* (decrees, the war council, your
  hosts) and *Houses* (honour, hostage, match, attaint; a lord's neighbours
  and the throne).
- **Realm**: the seed and fate of this realm, Reforge (a new realm, ending
  this one), the parchment map, day and night, labels, watch mode, the great
  houses and the line of succession (click a name to inspect it).
- **Overlays**: territories, tongues, trade, land and property value, land
  use, production and stores, prosperity, plague, unrest.
- **Kingdom accounts**: the realm's ledger: the treasury, the great houses,
  household purses, flows across the realm's border, and each town's balance
  sheet.
- **Annals**: the chronicle, filtered by **All, Crown, War, Trade, Fates**;
  export it as a text file or copy a link to this realm.
- **Rates** and **Acts**: Providence's dials and deeds (plague, dragon, fire,
  flood…). Both are sealed while you rule.
- **Plan**, **Save**, **Advisor**, **Help**, and **Petitions** when one waits.

## Taxes and dues

To raise or lower taxes, rule, then open **Menu → Crown → Overview**: the
**crown tax rate** (5–30%) is the hearth tax and the tolls on trade throughout
the realm; a slider and a box set it. A great house sets its **tenant dues**
there instead (50–160% of customary dues). Heavier rates fill the purse and
breed unrest, and sour the houses; the Kingdom accounts show what comes in.

## Cards

Click anything on the map, or a name in a card or the annals, to open its
card. A card is compact beside what it shows; **Expand** gives the whole sheet
(accounts, stores and prices, buildings, households, muster), **Minimize**
returns, **Close** or `Esc` dismisses it. A town you govern has **Details** and
**Orders** (relief grain, the watch, levies, walls, quarantine, works,
storage); a host of yours has its orders (march, objectives, supply road,
raft, split, join, hold, disband).

A toll house's card shows its toll, for whom it is taken, its keeper's pay and
its takings this year and last; a town's card lists its toll houses; a road's
card says who keeps it and what its mending cost last year against its tolls.

To march a host (an army): open its card, choose **Orders → Order a march**,
then click a town, an enemy host to attack, or open ground to camp.

## Choosing a place on the map

Some orders end in a place: a march, a road, a site, an act of Providence.
A bar at the foot of the screen says what to click; **Cancel** or `Esc` gives
it up. A road asks twice, for each end. An advisor answers with `game:place`.

## Petitions

When you rule, the court brings questions with a deadline: each names who and
where it touches, and its answers with their price. The game pauses when one
arrives (you can turn that off in the court). If you stay silent, the council
or your steward decides on the date shown.

## Plan with the brush

Choose a brush (Street, Square, Wall, Stop up a street, Pull down), drag on
the map, read the surveyors' price, then **Commission** or **Discard**.
**Done** closes the brush.

## The advisor's view of the screen

An advisor sees the same controls by name, `scope:command:argument`, such as
`crown:h-honour:3` (honour house 3) or `card:s-relief:5` (relief grain for town
5). The scopes are `hud`, `menu`, `crown`, `rates`, `acts`, `over`, `world`,
`annals`, and while they are open `card`, `petition`, `plan`, `context` (the
card's frame: close, expand, minimize), `info`, `event`, `legend`, `place` and
`dialog`. A closed panel still lists its controls. Names do not change from day
to day; each control says its cost, why it is disabled, what it acts on, and
any risk (Reforge and loading a game need the player's word). A press answers
when the game has answered: with its reply and the annals it wrote.
